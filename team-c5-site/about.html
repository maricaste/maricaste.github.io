Data critique

What the dataset includes

Temporary international visitor arrivals to the U.S. from 2000 to 2026, drawn from I-94 admission records and organized by country of residence and month, with air-only categories for Mexico and Canada and a separate sheet of year-over-year percentage change.

What it cannot reveal

Why people traveled, where they went in the U.S., and how many came for the tournament. It also leaves out travel to the other two host countries and some Mexican visitors within the frontier zone.

Origins and ontology

The NTTO, part of the Department of Commerce’s International Trade Administration, compiled the data from CBP I-94 records, Statistics Canada’s International Travel Survey, and Mexican agencies (INEGI and Banco de México). Funding for this specific dataset is not publicly disclosed. Sorting travelers by country of residence and month gives a U.S. government view of travel, and reduces fans to counts.
